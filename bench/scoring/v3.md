# Pac-Man Bake-off: Rescore (v3)

Site scores now use the v4 rule (`v4.md`), which adds visual accuracy and arcade extras on top of the v3 scores below. The v3 rule and table are unchanged.

Rescored 2026-10-13 from the v2 marks. No game was replayed or re-audited. Every card's v2 marks and notes (`score_notes` in `entries/meta.json`, and the rows in `v2.md`) are kept as they were. Only `score` changes.

**Why:** the user decided that minor Controls and Sound bugs cost too much in v2. Examples are a missing key-repeat filter, a key that double-fires, or notes that keep playing after pause. These are edge cases on a game that plays well. Under v2 they could cost as much as a ghost-AI flaw. v3 makes them cheap and leaves every other weight alone.

## Rule

v3 score = v2 score + **2** for each Controls or Sound check marked minor (⚠️). This applies only to cards with **no major (❌) mark** in any check.

- **Unchanged:** ok and major marks, minor marks in Ghosts, Pac-Man stuck and Maze, the category weights (Controls 20, Ghosts 25, Pac-Man stuck 20, Maze 20, Sound 15), and the low cap for games with no working enemies or a Pac-Man that can't move.
- **Same rule for every card.** The refund comes from the marks alone, not from re-reading the notes, so a missing feature marked minor gets the same refund as a minor bug.
- **Cards with a major mark keep their v2 score.** v2 did not score those by adding up deductions. It placed them with the low cap and with direct comparisons between broken builds, such as claude-haiku-5-5 below deepseek-v4.1-flash, or grok-bot-2026-10-07 below muse-spark-1.3. Their minor Controls and Sound marks had no separate weight to refund. Some of those marks describe sound that never plays (kimi-k2.7-code).
- **Why 2:** v2 has no per-mark formula, so its cost for a minor was measured. Ten cards have no major mark and no minor outside Controls and Sound. Regressing their v2 scores on how many Controls/Sound minors they have gives **2.3 points per minor mark**. The means are 97.5 with no minors (2 cards), 91.75 with one (4) and 92.0 with two (4). Refunding 2 brings a minor Controls/Sound mark down to about 0.3 points, roughly a seventh of v2. The rest of each v2 score is the reviewer's judgement of the build within its marks (for example opus-5-5 99 against fable-5-1 96, both all ok). v3 keeps that judgement as it is.
- **New entries:** score the v2 way (`audit.md`, the playtest and the `v2.md` calibration table) to get a v2 score, then apply this rule. Record both in the entry's row below.

## What moved

- **Top group:** builds with two minor Controls/Sound marks and nothing else (claude-fable-5, gemini-4-argon, gpt-6.1-sol) gain 4 and close up on the all-ok cards. claude-fable-5 now ties claude-opus-5-5 at 99. gemini-4-argon (97) ties claude-sonnet-5-5 for 3rd and moves above claude-fable-5-1 (96) and grok-4.7 (96).
- **gemini-4-argon is not 1st or 2nd under this rule.** claude-fable-5 has the same two minor marks and a v2 score 2 points higher (95 against 93), so any refund based on marks alone keeps it ahead. claude-opus-5-5 has no minor marks, so a refund can't close that gap. To rank gemini-4-argon higher, its v2 score itself would have to be re-judged. Changing how minor marks are weighted can't do it.
- **Middle:** cards with a Ghosts or Maze minor (grok-4.6, gpt-6-sol, qwen3.8-flash) fall back against builds whose only minors were Controls/Sound. gpt-5.6-sol (one Controls minor) drops below grok-bot-2026-10-08 and muse-wonder (two each).
- **From gpt-6-sol (83) down, nothing changes.** The lowest refunded card is glm-5.3-flash at 90, so gpt-6-sol, qwen3.8-flash and every card with a major mark keep both score and rank.
