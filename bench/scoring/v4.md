# Pac-Man Bake-off: Rescore (v4)

Rescored 2026-10-13. v4 keeps every v3 score and adds two things the earlier rubric never looked at: how closely the game **looks** like the 1980 arcade Pac-Man, and whether it has the arcade's **authentic extras** (bonus fruit, attract screen and so on). Marks and notes from v2 (`score_notes` in `entries/meta.json`) are unchanged. Only `score` changes.

**Why:** the user found that cards which play well but look wrong scored too high. Examples: grok cards with odd-looking ghosts; gemini-4-argon with a tinted frame around the maze and slightly wrong maze corners. Authentic touches such as a working bonus fruit or the CHARACTER / NICKNAME attract screen earned nothing.

## Rule

**v4 score = v3 score − visual deduction + bonuses**, rounded half up and capped at 100. It applies only to cards with **no major (❌) mark** in any v2 check, the same gate as v3.

### Visual accuracy (deductions, max 22)

Judged against the arcade screen: pure black playfield; thin blue outlined walls with rounded corners and a double outer border, never filled blocks; the 28×31 arcade layout with tunnels and a pink ghost-house door; small square peach dots and large round blinking power pellets; a yellow Pac-Man with an opening and closing wedge mouth; ghosts with a dome head, straight sides, a ragged 3–4 point skirt and white eyes with blue pupils, in red, pink, cyan and orange; HUD with 1UP and HIGH SCORE at the top, lives bottom-left, level fruit bottom-right, a blocky arcade font and a yellow READY! at the start, and nothing else on screen.

| Check | Minor | Major | Minor means | Major means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 Maze & background | −3 | −6 | reads as the arcade maze with 1–2 noticeable deviations (tinted fill or glow, wrong corners, wall thickness or colour, single outer border, small layout changes) | doesn't read as the arcade maze (filled wall blocks, different layout, non-blue walls) or 3+ deviations |
| V2 Ghosts | −3 | −6 | clearly arcade ghosts with one detail off (skirt, eyes, colour) | shape doesn't read as the arcade ghost (blob, skewed polygon, side-profile, missing skirt or eyes) |
| V3 Pac-Man | −2 | −4 | one detail off (mouth, colour, extra eye) | doesn't read as arcade Pac-Man |
| V4 Dots & power pellets | −1 | −2 | one detail off (round dots, wrong colour or size) | several off, or pellets missing |
| V5 HUD & framing | −2 | −4 | 1–2 deviations (non-arcade font, LEVEL label, extra help text, page chrome) | 3+ deviations (toolbar of buttons, frames or glow round the playfield, side panels, non-black surround, missing 1UP/HIGH SCORE or fruit row) |

A check is `na` (no deduction) only when nothing renders because of a crash v2 already priced (minimax-m3).

### Authentic extras (bonuses, max 8)

| Bonus | Points | Counts only if |
|---|---|---|
| B1 Bonus fruit | +3 full, +1 partial | full: a fruit appears in the maze during play and eating it raises the score. partial: it appears but eating it doesn't work. A fruit icon only in the HUD scores 0. |
| B2 Attract screen | +2 full, +1 partial | full: the arcade CHARACTER / NICKNAME roster (or an attract demo that plays itself). partial: a title with one arcade element beyond the logo (ghost line-up, point table, INSERT COIN). A plain logo or "press start" overlay scores 0. |
| B3 Retro rendering | +1 | Pac-Man and ghosts are pixel-art bitmaps, or the HUD uses an arcade bitmap font. A CRT filter alone doesn't count. |
| B4 Level-clear flash | +1 | the maze walls visibly flash (white/blue) when a level is cleared. |
| B5 Intermission | +1 | an arcade cutscene plays between levels. No entry has one. |

### Why these numbers

- **Visual weights follow how much of the screen each element is.** The maze and the ghosts dominate the screen and carry the user's two examples, so they cost the most (3/6). Pac-Man is one sprite (2/4). Dots are tiny (1/2). The HUD is around the edges (2/4).
- **Big enough to reorder the top.** v3's top 14 span 90–99. A major on the maze or ghosts (−6) or a full fruit (+3) moves a card several places. The worst-looking working builds lose 17–18 points.
- **Bonuses are capped below the worst visual deduction** (8 against 22), so a game can't buy back looking wrong with extras.
- **Why cards with a major mark keep their v3 score:** the same reason as v3. v2 placed those cards with a low cap and direct comparisons between broken builds, not by adding up deductions. Deducting up to 22 points from cards at 2–20 would pin most of them at 0 and erase that order. Some of their bonuses could only be reached by teleporting Pac-Man past a movement bug (gemini-3.8-flash, muse-spark-1.3). Their findings are recorded below for the record.

## Method

1. **Screenshots.** `bench/playtest/screenshots.py` loaded every entry in headless Chromium (900×1000, 2× scale) and saved five shots: attract at 1 s and 6 s with no input, start (Enter, then any Start button), and play at about 5 s and 14 s with a random held-arrow bot.
2. **Blind visual pass.** Each card's start and play shots were trimmed, joined side by side and renamed to a random `card-NNN` ID. Four judge agents, each given 19 IDs, the rubric and a pixel-perfect arcade reference screen (Wikimedia Commons, "Pac-Man gameplay (1x pixel-perfect recreation).png"), marked V1–V5 and B3. Every card was judged by two judges who didn't see each other's marks or the model names.
3. **Reconciliation.** A card's deduction is the mean of its two judges' deductions per check (so a minor/major split on the maze costs 4.5). B3 needs both judges to say yes. One systematic split was removed: one judge marked ghosts or Pac-Man "undersized" as minor and another explicitly didn't, since nearly every clone draws sprites about one tile wide. Size-only marks (19 of them) were set to ok for every card. The marks with another reason, such as a glow or an eye dot, stand.
4. **Bonus probes.** Agents read each entry's source and then made each bonus happen in headless Chromium. For fruit they set the game's own dots-eaten counter to 69 (or the game's own threshold minus one) so the game's code spawned the fruit on the next real dot, walked Pac-Man onto it and recorded the score before and after. Where state was sealed in a closure, a scratch copy got one added line exposing it (`window.__probe = code => eval(code)`); no game logic was changed. Level clears were forced by leaving a few dots in Pac-Man's path. Attract screens were captured at 1, 6, 12 and 20 s with no input.

## What moved

- **Top:** claude-opus-5-5 and claude-fable-5 both have near-arcade visuals (−5: maze without side walls or with double-stroked inner walls, non-arcade font) and every verified extra except retro (+6), so both hit the 100 cap. gemini-4-argon loses 7 for its CRT-textured maze with stray corner loops and its toolbar, glowing frame and help footer, and gains 7 for fruit, the full roster, pixel-art sprites and the flash. It stays at 97 and is now 3rd on its own.
- **grok cards drop most.** Both blind judges marked grok-4.7's and grok-4.6's ghosts major (skewed polygons and side-profile blobs) and their mazes major (filled blocks; dashed segments on a navy field). grok-4.7 falls 96 → 83 and grok-4.6 91 → 78. grok-4.7-medium (−6) and grok-bot-2026-10-08 (−8.5) look much closer to the arcade and land on 91.
- **Web-page styling costs.** Builds wrapped in a site layout with side panels, buttons and progress bars (gpt-6-astra, gpt-6-sol, gpt-6.1-sol) lose 9.5–12 on visuals. gpt-6-sol has no fruit and falls 83 → 72.
- **qwen3.8-flash** (no fruit; a different maze with no proper ghost house on a navy field; round yellow dots) falls 76 → 63, below three cards with a major mark (qwen3.8-max 74, deepseek-v4.1-flash 72, claude-haiku-5-5 70) whose scores the gate leaves alone.
- **No intermission** is implemented in any of the 38 entries.

## New entries

Score the v2 way and apply v3, then: run `bench/playtest/screenshots.py`, have two blind judges mark V1–V5 and B3 against the reference using this file, and verify B1/B2/B4/B5 in headless Chromium the way the Method section describes. If the card has no major mark, apply the rule above and add its rows to both tables, marked *(added YYYY-MM-DD)*.
